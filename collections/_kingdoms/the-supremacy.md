The Supremacy is the largest and most powerful nation on the continent of **Morwen** — a vast, disciplined empire built upon the teachings of the **Oracle**, whose divine visions guide the will of the state. To its people, the Supremacy is not merely a nation but destiny itself: a chosen civilization charged with bringing order to the chaos beyond its borders. Its banners of white and crimson fly from every fortress, symbolizing purity through control and unity through faith.

Ruled by the **Children of the Oracle**, a theocratic council claiming direct descent from the first Oracle’s line, the Supremacy blends religion, bureaucracy, and military might into a single, unbreakable system. To defy the Oracle is to defy the divine — and to defy the divine is treason.

## **History**
The Supremacy was founded nearly a millennium ago, when the first **Oracle of Vaelion** arose from among the desert tribes of central Morwen. Claiming visions from the “Voice Beyond Flame,” the Oracle united the warring city-states through prophecy and fire, declaring that the world must be “tempered in order, as iron in the forge.”  

Over centuries, this doctrine evolved into a state religion — the **Doctrine of Ascendant Law** — where divine order and human will are one. Through conquest and assimilation, the Supremacy expanded its borders until only the most hostile lands resisted: the frozen north of **Njorath**, the burning deserts of **Dun’Rhael**, and the volcanic wastes of the **Ashen Marches**.  

Despite its reach, rebellion simmers in the south and east. The secession of **Vardessa** marked the first great fracture in living memory — a wound the Supremacy swears to heal, no matter the cost.

## **Geography**
The Supremacy spans the heartlands of Morwen — a land of arid plains, fertile river valleys, and windswept highlands. The **Stormbarrier Range** defines its eastern border with **Drenvold**, while to the north the lands rise into icy tundra bordering **Njorath**.  

In the south, the terrain descends into the cracked badlands near **Dun’Rhael** and the scorched fields that touch the **Ashen Marches**. The capital, **Vaelion**, sits near the continent’s center — an architectural marvel of alabaster stone and gilded domes, encircled by the **Circle Roads**, a network of perfectly measured highways that radiate like spokes across the empire.

## **Economy**
The Supremacy’s economy is vast, centralized, and tightly controlled. Agricultural output from the central plains feeds the populace, while trade and tithes from conquered lands sustain the capital.  

It is also home to the **Forge Guilds**, an alliance of state-run foundries producing arms, armor, and war machines unmatched on the continent. Its naval yards at **Caedros** and **Tharnis** ensure command of the seas, while caravans flow endlessly along the **Ascendant Roads**, bringing salt from Dun’Rhael, metals from Drenvold, and grain from Vardessa.

All trade is regulated through the **Office of Measure**, a bureaucracy famed (and feared) for its precision. Counterfeiting, hoarding, or unauthorized trade are considered heresies against the divine order.

## **Demographics**
The Supremacy is a **multiracial empire**, though **humans** form the clear majority (around 70%). **Thaldrim** engineers and shipwrights are valued within the navy and foundries, **Solari** scholars serve as translators and desert scouts, and **Emberborn** mercenaries are often found in its border legions.  

The government enforces a strict hierarchy: citizens of pure Supremacy bloodlines hold the highest ranks, while nonhuman subjects are often restricted to guild service or military contracts. Officially, all are “Children of Order” — but in practice, prejudice burns quietly beneath the surface.

## **Culture**
The Supremacy’s culture is one of devotion, precision, and unshakable belief. Every citizen’s life follows the rhythm of the **Hours of Light**, a cycle of prayer, work, and reflection dictated by the Oracle’s teachings.  

Art and architecture exalt symmetry and flame — cathedrals of light rising beside cold marble fortresses. Music is solemn and mathematical, built on perfect intervals meant to reflect cosmic harmony.  

The **Children of the Oracle**, who rule from Vaelion, are both priests and administrators. Their word is absolute. Beneath them, the **Flameward Orders** enforce doctrine, burning heresy wherever it takes root.  

While outsiders see only tyranny, the people of the Supremacy see purpose. To them, the world beyond their borders — fractured, untamed, and chaotic — is a wound waiting to be healed by their guiding flame.

## **Relations**
- **Njorath**: Mutual respect tempered by cold distrust. The Supremacy views Njorath’s isolation as defiance of divine order.  
- **Drenvold**: Separated by the Stormbarrier Range; trade is limited, and relations are strained but stable.  
- **Vardessa**: Considered a rebellious province and an ongoing warfront.  
- **Dun’Rhael**: Viewed as heretics bound to superstition, though the Supremacy maintains fragile trade for desert resources.  
- **Ashen Marches**: Hostile territory of the Emberborn — seen as an affront to divine order and under constant surveillance.
