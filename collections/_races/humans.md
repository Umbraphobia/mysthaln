Humans are the most numerous and widespread of all peoples, yet also the most divided. Their origins have long been lost — even the oldest records of the Supremacy claim no homeland. Wherever they go, they build, adapt, and reshape the world in their image. To some, they are pioneers. To others, usurpers.

## Personality & Behavior

Driven and ambitious, humans are rarely content to remain still. They are dreamers and conquerors both — capable of great compassion or ruthless cruelty depending on whose banner they follow. While other races see them as inconsistent, humans view themselves as versatile: able to become whatever the world demands of them.

## Appearance

Human appearance varies wildly by region, from the sun-bronzed traders of the southern deserts to the pale, ice-scarred wanderers of the far north. Their adaptability is their hallmark; even among the Supremacy’s ranks, no two human faces tell the same story.

## Culture

The largest concentration of humans resides within the Supremacy, a powerful realm ruled by faith and control. Yet countless smaller nations exist — city-states, kingdoms, nomadic tribes — each shaped by their environment and beliefs. Some humans serve under the Children of the Oracle, bound by law and beacon; others flee that order entirely, carving free lives in distant lands.

Human culture values ingenuity and ambition, often blending the traditions of other peoples into their own. Their guilds, fleets, and armies can be found in nearly every corner of the world, whether as conquerors, explorers, or exiles.

Many scholars whisper that humans’ forgotten homeland still exists — a lost cradle buried beneath time — but if it does, none alive can name it.
