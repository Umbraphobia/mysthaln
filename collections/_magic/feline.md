*Masters of Agility, Stealth, and Predatory Precision*

Those bonded with cats—domestic or wild—embody the solitary elegance and lethality of their feline companions. Feline Bond casters are swift, elusive, and precise, excelling at stealth, climbing, acrobatics, and devastating single-target strikes. Their magic enhances reflexes, balance, and the instinctive cunning of the hunt, allowing them to dominate with speed and grace where brute force would fail.

---

## **Abilities Granted by the Feline Bond**

**Quickstep** – Instantly accelerate to high speed for a brief moment.  
**Pounceburst** – Leap an incredible distance and strike with surprise force.  
**Reflex Surge** – Dramatically heighten reaction time, improving dodges and parries.  
**Whisker Sense** – Detect incoming attacks from any direction at close range.  
**Nine Lives** – Temporarily gain a surge of luck, avoiding the next fatal blow.  
**Cat’s Grace** – Land any fall safely, even from great heights.  
**Silent Paws** – Move without sound, even over loud or unstable surfaces.  
**Shadowmeld** – Blend into dim light or shadow, becoming nearly invisible while still.  
**Wallclaw** – Climb vertical surfaces with incredible grip.  
**Ledgewalker** – Move with effortless feline balance, maintaining sure footing even in precarious or shifting environments.  
**Tetherclaw** – Leap onto a creature and cling to it, restricting movement.  
**Finisher's Instinct** – Deal bonus damage to wounded or fleeing targets.  
**Clawstorm** – Unleash a rapid flurry of short-range attacks.  
**Throatseeker** – Increased critical chance when striking unaware or distracted foes.  
**Shared Senses** – Briefly merge senses with your feline for heightened perception and reflexes.  
**Predatory Rush** – Your speed increases as your feline grows more excited or agitated in battle.  
**Slipstrike** – Perform a lightning-fast feint to confuse your opponent before striking.
