*Masters of Teleportation, Light, and Spatial Distortion*

The Butterfly and Moth Bond grants its wielder unparalleled control over space, motion, and ephemeral light. Those linked to these delicate yet otherworldly creatures can slip between moments, fold distance into ribbons, and vanish like drifting motes on the wind. Their magic is characterized by fluttering movement, soft luminescence, and unpredictable distortions of reality.  

Butterfly/Moth casters are elusive, evasive, and impossibly hard to pin down—ideal for scouts, infiltrators, escape artists, and anyone who prefers finesse over brute force.

---

## **Abilities Granted by the Butterfly & Moth Bond**

**Blink** – Teleport to any spot within a short radius.  
**Anchor** – Imbue a specific location to mark it for future teleportation.  
**Swap** – Instantly change places with your bonded butterfly or moth.  
**Teleport** – Instantly teleport to an anchored position.  
**Portal** – Create a person-sized portal for a short duration linking to an anchored position.  
**Shift** – Return to a previous spot within a short timeframe.  
**Gate** – Create a large but short-lived portal leading to an anchored position.  
**Push** – Send an object instantly to an anchored position.  
**Warp** – Send a creature to an anchored position.  

**Flicker Trap** – Imbue an item that teleports whoever touches it to an anchored position.  
**Afterimage** – Leave behind illusionary afterimages that confuse and mislead foes.  
**Phase** – Partially step out of reality, becoming difficult to strike for a brief time.  
**Send** – Make a target incorporeal, preventing them from physically interacting with anything for a short duration.  
**Fraywing Burst** – Release a burst of glittering dust that blinds enemies briefly.  
**Fading Touch** – Touch a creature, granting them a single-use Blink within the next round.  
**Dimensional Fold** – Briefly collapse space to allow you and allies to walk a few steps that cover great distance.  
**Sunderstep** – Blink with violent spatial distortion, knocking back creatures near both departure and arrival points.

## Ability Details

All below abilities have:
- Type: Butterfly & Moth Bond
- User Requirements: Bonded Butterfly or Moth

---

- **Name:** Blink
- **Cost:** 0
- **Target Range:** CS
- **Target Type:** Self
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Instantly teleport to any unoccupied space within S.
    - You may use this skill even if you are within C of an opponent.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - You cannot teleport into Impassable Terrain.
- **Learn Requirements:** No Trait REQ

---

- **Name:** Swap
- **Cost:** 1
- **Target Range:** S
- **Target Type:** Self
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Instantly exchange positions with your bonded butterfly or moth.
    - Both you and your bonded creature must be within S of one another.
    - You may use this ability even if you are within C of an opponent.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - You cannot swap into Impassable Terrain or an occupied space.
    - If your bonded butterfly or moth is unable to occupy your space (due to terrain or obstruction), this ability fails with no effect.
- **Learn Requirements:** Intuition 2

---

- **Name:** Anchor
- **Cost:** 2
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Space
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Imbue a single unoccupied space you are touching with a shimmering wing-mark, designating it as an Anchor point.
    - You may have only one active Anchor at a time. Creating a new Anchor removes the previous one.
    - The Anchor remains for up to 24 hours, or until you take a Long Rest.
    - The Anchored space must remain unoccupied to be a valid teleport destination.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 2, Intuition 2

---

- **Name:** Teleport
- **Cost:** 2
- **Target Range:** Self
- **Target Type:** Self
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Instantly teleport to your active **Anchor**.
    - This teleport may be used regardless of distance, provided the Anchored space remains valid.
    - You may use this ability even if you are within C of an opponent.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - You cannot teleport into Impassable Terrain or an occupied space.
    - If your Anchor is no longer valid, this ability fails with no effect.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 3, Intuition 3

---

- **Name:** Portal
- **Cost:** 4
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Space
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Create a shimmering, person-sized portal in a single unoccupied space you are touching.
    - The portal links directly to your active **Anchor**.
    - For the next **2 rounds**, any creature may move through the portal, emerging into the Anchored space.
    - Moving through the portal counts as movement and ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - The Anchored space must remain unoccupied to receive creatures exiting the portal.
    - If the Anchor becomes invalid or is removed, the portal immediately collapses.
    - You cannot create a portal if you do not have an active Anchor.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 4, Engineering 3

---

- **Name:** Shift
- **Cost:** 2
- **Target Range:** Self
- **Target Type:** Self
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Instantly return to a space you previously occupied within the last **2 rounds**.
    - The destination space must be unoccupied and valid for your character.
    - You may use this ability even if you are within C of an opponent.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - You cannot return to a space that has become Impassable.
    - If no valid previous space exists within the timeframe, this ability fails with no effect.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 2, Survival 2

---

- **Name:** Gate
- **Cost:** 6
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Space
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Create a large, shimmering gate in a single unoccupied space you are touching.
    - The gate links directly to your active **Anchor**.
    - For the next **1 round**, any number of creatures may pass through the gate, emerging into the Anchored space in an order of their choosing.
    - Creatures of up to **Large** size may pass through the gate, at the GM’s discretion.
    - Moving through the gate counts as movement and ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - The Anchored space must remain unoccupied and valid to receive creatures.
    - If the Anchor becomes invalid or is removed, the gate immediately collapses.
    - You cannot create a gate if you do not have an active Anchor.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 4, Engineering 4

---

- **Name:** Push
- **Cost:** 1
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Space
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):**
    - Instantly teleport a single unattended object from the targeted space to your active **Anchor**.
    - The object must be no larger than what you could reasonably carry with two hands.
    - The Anchored space must be unoccupied and able to contain the object.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - You cannot push objects into Impassable Terrain or through solid barriers that would prevent placement.
    - If the Anchor becomes invalid or is removed before resolution, this ability fails with no effect.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 2, Power 2

---

- **Name:** Warp
- **Cost:** 6
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Space
- **Roll Details:** +0 Butterfly & Moth Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):**
    - Instantly teleport a single creature from the targeted space to your active **Anchor**.
    - The target creature must be within C of the targeted space at the time of activation.
    - The Anchored space must be unoccupied and capable of containing the creature.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain.
    - A creature moved by Warp does not provoke reactions based on movement.
    - You cannot warp a creature into Impassable Terrain.
    - If the Anchor becomes invalid or is removed before resolution, this ability fails with no effect.
- **Learn Requirements:** Knowledge 4, Power 3
