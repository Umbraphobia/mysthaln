Those who bond with bears awaken the ancient strength of the earth itself. The connection grants immense physical resilience, an unshakable presence, and the ability to shape stone with both finesse and overwhelming force. As the bond deepens, the bear companion grows wiser and more grounded, embodying the patience and power of the mountains.

Stone magic emphasizes **endurance**, **protection**, and **raw seismic might**, making Bear-bonded casters the bulwarks of any battle.

---

### **Abilities Granted by the Bear Bond**

- **Stoneskin** - Harden your body like granite, drastically increasing physical resistance.
- **Stone Guard** - Raise a thick stone barrier from the earth to block incoming attacks.
- **Earthen Bulwark** - Surround yourself with a dome of densely packed stone for near-total protection.
- **Mountain’s Stance** - Root yourself in place, becoming immovable and resistant to knockback.
- **Ironfur** - Coat your bear companion or allies in protective mineral layers.
- **Bedrock Recovery** - Slowly regenerate vitality while standing on natural stone or earth.
- **Earthquake** - Shake the ground violently, knocking enemies prone and disrupting formations.
- **Meteorshower** - Call down small stone meteors to strike a targeted area.
- **Boulder Throw** - Conjure and hurl a massive slab of stone at enemies.
- **Shardstorm** - Erupt sharp stone fragments from the ground to slice across a chosen zone.
- **Raise Pillar** - Create a rising stone pillar to lift allies, reach heights, or block paths.
- **Tremorsense** - Detect movement and vibrations through the earth, revealing hidden foes.
- **Stonecall** - Summon loose stones and minerals to orbit around you in a defensive aura.
- **Terraforge** - Shape natural stone into tools, weapons, barriers, or temporary structures.
- **Heart of the Mountain** - Become nearly invulnerable for a short time at the cost of mobility.
- **Avalanche** - Unleash a cascading wall of collapsing stone and rubble.
- **Stone Spirit Bind** - Empower your bear companion with the spirit of the mountain, greatly increasing its size and power.

---

The Bear Bond is the magic of **endurance, patience, and unstoppable force**—a living testament to the mountain’s strength.
