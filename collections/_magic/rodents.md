*Masters of Rot, Decay, and Tenacious Survival*

Those who bond with rodents gain mastery over rot and necrosis, shaping decay as both a weapon and a tool. Their bond reflects the tenacity, resourcefulness, and survival-driven instincts of creatures who thrive in the shadows, carving resilience from hardship and strength from ruin.

---

## **Abilities Granted by the Rodent Bond**

**Rotbite** – Inflict a rapidly decaying wound that spreads mild necrotic damage over time.  
**Gnawing Decay** – Targeted object or armor slowly breaks down as if chewed by invisible teeth.  
**Putrefy** – Cause organic material to rot instantly, turning food, wood, or corpses into sludge.  
**Moldburst** – Release a burst of fast-growing mold that blinds or chokes enemies in a small area.  
**Carrion Bloom** – Animate a patch of rotting ground that lashes at enemies with fungal tendrils.  
**Filth Fever** – Infect a target with a debilitating sickness that saps strength and accuracy.  
**Toxic Gnarl** – Coat your or your rodent’s claws/teeth with an infectious necrotic toxin.  
**Septic Cloud** – Summon a rancid vapor that obscures vision and induces coughing fits.  
**Spoil** – Corrupt a nearby water or food source, making it temporarily harmful.  
**Burrowborn Resilience** – Gain temporary resistance to poison, disease, and necrotic damage.  
**Scrapper’s Endurance** – Temporarily boosts stamina and pain resistance.  
**Scavenger’s Vitality** – Heal slightly whenever eating food.  
**Resistant Nest** – Create a protective zone that reduces poison and disease effects for allies.  
**Rot** – Infest a small area with rot that slows enemies and lowers their defenses.  
**Fester Mark** – Mark a target so all necrotic or poison effects against them are intensified.  
**Nibblestep** – Move silently and swiftly through debris or cluttered terrain.  
**Scrap Sense** – Detect weak points in decaying structures, traps, or barriers.
