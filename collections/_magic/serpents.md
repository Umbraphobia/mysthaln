*Masters of Silver Tongues, Subtle Influence, and Serpentine Deception*

Those bonded to serpents wield magic woven from whispers, emotions, and subtle manipulations. Their tongues carry charm like venom, their eyes coil with hypnotic power, and even in battle they bend minds with feints and illusions. The Serpent Bond excels at social magic, psychological disruption, and battlefield misdirection.

---

## **Abilities Granted by the Serpent Bond**

- **Serpent’s Guile** – Plant a subtle suggestion in a target’s mind that feels like their own idea.  
- **Velvet Voice** – Smooth over anger or fear, calming hostile emotions.  
- **Heartcoil** – Momentarily enthrall a creature with hypnotic charm.  
- **Soothevenom** – Replace a creature’s panic or rage with tranquility.  
- **Charmsmoke** – Exhale a faint illusionary mist that makes those within it more persuadable.  
- **Empathic Bind** – Read a creature’s strongest current emotion, gaining advantage in influencing them.  
- **Mood Twist** – Sharply flip a creature’s emotional state (fear to bravery, hostility to confusion, etc.).  
- **Forked Tongue** – Temporarily speak in two voices at once, sowing doubt or conflicting messages.  
- **Shifting Persona** – Temporarily adopt an aura that causes people to perceive you as a specific type of person (leader, friend, authority).  
- **Slick Feint** – Create a false verbal cue or gesture that misleads enemies during battle.  
- **Doubt** – Plant a sudden wave of uncertainty in a target, causing hesitation.  
- **Venom Thought** – Inject a brief intrusive thought or distraction into a target’s mind.  
- **Hypnotic Coil** – Use spiraling magical patterns in your eyes to hold a creature’s focus.  
- **Serpent Dream** – Plant a symbolic vision or fleeting hallucination to influence a target’s mood.  
- **Shed** – Temporarily purge yourself of lies or mental influence, refreshing clarity.  
- **Frightmask** – Briefly conjure an illusionary serpent aura that intimidates foes.  
- **Coaxing Lure** – Tempt a creature to move toward you or a chosen point.
