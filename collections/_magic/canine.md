*Masters of Leadership, Pack Synergy, and Frontline Combat*

Those bonded with dogs or wolves channel the spirit of the pack—commanding allies, protecting companions, and tearing through foes with coordinated ferocity. A Canine Bond wielder is the heart of any battle group, leading from the front with courage, instinct, and unwavering loyalty. Their magic emphasizes teamwork, tactical dominance, and brutal close-quarters combat.

---

## **Abilities Granted by the Canine Bond**

**Rally the Pack** – Boost the morale and combat effectiveness of all nearby allies.  
**Alpha’s Command** – Issue a shouted command that grants allies bonus movement.  
**Lead the Hunt** – Mark a target; all allies deal extra damage when attacking it.  
**Lunge Assault** – Dash into melee and strike all enemies in your path.  
**Pack Fury** – Increase melee damage when fighting adjacent to an ally.  
**Alpha’s Advance** – Gain forward momentum that pushes enemies back as you attack.  
**Rip and Tear** – A savage melee attack that deals increased damage to wounded foes.  
**Pack Shield** – Automatically intercept an attack aimed at an adjacent ally.  
**Scent of Danger** – Detect invisible enemies or anticipate ambushes.  
**Loyal Bond** – Temporarily transfer damage from an ally to yourself to keep them alive.  
**Trailmarker** – Leave a scent-based magical trail that allies can follow unerringly.  
**Pack Mend** – Minor healing distributed among all nearby allies.  
**Blood Track** – Track an enemy by scent over vast distances, even through magic.  
**Pack Collapse** – Designate a target; all allies can immediately move closer to it.  
**Rallying Charge** – Lead a group charge that boosts ally movement and impact damage.  
**Shared Prowess** – Temporarily gain one of your canine’s physical attributes (speed, reflexes, scent).  
**Dual Fang** – Combine your melee strike with your canine’s bite for a single devastating combo attack.
