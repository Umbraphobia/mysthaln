*Masters of Leadership, Pack Synergy, and Frontline Combat*

Those bonded with dogs or wolves channel the spirit of the pack—commanding allies, protecting companions, and tearing through foes with coordinated ferocity. A Canine Bond wielder is the heart of any battle group, leading from the front with courage, instinct, and unwavering loyalty. Their magic emphasizes teamwork, tactical dominance, and brutal close-quarters combat.

---

## **Abilities Granted by the Canine Bond**

**Rally the Pack** – Boost the morale and combat effectiveness of all nearby allies.  
**Pack Fury** – Increase melee damage when fighting adjacent to an ally.  
**Pack Mend** – Minor healing distributed among all nearby allies.  
**Pack Shield** – Automatically intercept an attack aimed at an adjacent ally.  
**Lead the Hunt** – Mark a target; all allies deal extra damage when attacking it.  
**Pack Collapse** – Designate a target; all allies can immediately move closer to it.  
**Rip and Tear** – A savage melee attack that deals increased damage to wounded foes.  
**Dual Fang** – Combine your melee strike with your canine’s bite for a single devastating combo attack.  
**Loyal Bond** – Temporarily transfer damage from an ally to yourself to keep them alive.  

**Alpha’s Command** – Issue a shouted command that grants allies bonus movement.  
**Lunge Assault** – Dash into melee and strike all enemies in your path.  
**Alpha’s Advance** – Gain forward momentum that pushes enemies back as you attack.  
**Scent of Danger** – Detect invisible enemies or anticipate ambushes.  
**Trailmarker** – Leave a scent-based magical trail that allies can follow unerringly.  
**Blood Track** – Track an enemy by scent over vast distances, even through magic.  
**Rallying Charge** – Lead a group charge that boosts ally movement and impact damage.  
**Shared Prowess** – Temporarily gain one of your canine’s physical attributes (speed, reflexes, scent).

## Ability Details

All below abilities have:
- Type: Canine Bond
- Use Requirements: Bonded Canine

---

- **Name:** Rally the Pack
- **Cost:** 0
- **Target Range:** CS
- **Target Type:** Ally
- **Roll Details:** -1 Canine Focus Skill
    - All allies within S are considered part of your **Pack** until the end of combat or when no longer in M range.
    - Allies that become part of the Pack may immediately benefit from Pack-based abilities and effects.
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
- **Learn Requirements:** No Trait REQ

---

- **Name:** Pack Fury
- **Cost:** 1
- **Target Range:** Self
- **Target Type:** Ally
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Until the end of the current round, all members of your **Pack** deal **+1 Pure Damage** on all successful melee attacks.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 2, Power 2
- **Use Requirements:** A **Pack** created by Rally the Pack

---


- **Name:** Pack Shield
- **Cost:** 1
- **Target Range:** Self
- **Target Type:** Ally
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Until the end of the current round, all members of your **Pack** gain **+1 die** to their Combat Rolls when making defensive actions.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 2, Resistance 2
- **Use Requirements:** A **Pack** created by Rally the Pack

---

- **Name:** Pack Mend
- **Cost:** 6
- **Target Range:** Self
- **Target Type:** Ally
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - All members of your **Pack** recover **1 HP**.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 4, Survival 4
- **Use Requirements:** A **Pack** created by Rally the Pack

---

- **Name:** Lead the Hunt
- **Cost:** 1
- **Target Range:** CSML+
- **Target Type:** Enemy
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Designate a single enemy within range as your **Marked Prey**.
    - The marked target remains marked until the end of combat or until you mark a new target.
    - You may only have **one Marked Prey** at a time.
    - Marked Prey may be referenced by other Canine Bond abilities and effects.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 2, Acuity 2

---

- **Name:** Pack Collapse
- **Cost:** 3
- **Target Range:** Self
- **Target Type:** Ally
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - All members of your **Pack** may immediately move up to **S** toward your **Marked Prey**.
    - This movement must end closer to the Marked Prey than where it began.
    - This movement ignores Difficult Terrain but cannot pass through Impassable Terrain.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 3, Coordination 3
- **Use Requirements:** A **Marked Prey** created by Lead the Hunt

---

- **Name:** Rip and Tear
- **Cost:** 2
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Enemy
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** +0 Focus Damage
    - If the target is **Injured** or otherwise wounded, deal **+1 pure damage**.
    - If the target is your **Marked Prey**, deal **+1 pure damage**.
    - These bonuses stack.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 2, Power 2

---

- **Name:** Dual Fang
- **Cost:** 4
- **Target Range:** C
- **Target Type:** Enemy
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** +1 Focus Damage
    - If the target is your **Marked Prey**, inflict **1 Bleed**.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 4, Power 3

---

- **Name:** Loyal Bond
- **Cost:** 3
- **Target Range:** S
- **Target Type:** Ally
- **Roll Details:** +0 Canine Focus Skill
- **Effect (Success Only):** Defended
    - Choose a member of your **Pack** within range.
    - Take damage up to your current HP -1 and heal the chosen **Pack Member** by the same amount.
- **Learn Requirements:** Authority 3, Survival 3
- **Use Requirements:** A **Pack** created by Rally the Pack
