## Full List of Cities

| Population | Name | Notes / Flavor |
|------------|------|----------------|
| 26,000 | **Varketh** | The largest and oldest of the Marches. Known as *the First Forge*, said to be built around the crater where the Emberborn first heard the “voice of flame.” The de facto cultural heart of the Marches. |
| 25,000 (Seaport) | **Cinderval** | A massive port on the Ashen Sea where obsidian and slag glass are exported. Its harbors glow red at night from furnaces lining the cliffs. |
| 24,000 (Seaport) | **Orun's Gate** | A fortress-port city built on cooled lava shelves. Known for its warship foundries and tempestuous harbor winds. Rival to Cinderval. |
| 19,000 | **Thurakar** | Center of the *Cycle of Ash* faith. Built within a ring of five volcanoes known as the Ember Crown. Priests and pyromancers rule jointly here. |
| 13,000 | **Molkarin** | The “Molten Bastion.” A militarized city famous for its iron and basalt fortifications; its forges never cool. |
| 11,000 | **Ashirn** | Known as the “City of Breath,” where volcanic gases are harvested for alchemy. A spiritual, reclusive city with red-stained architecture. |
| 9,700 | **Vaerthas** | Founded on a plateau of cooled glass. Renowned for artisans who craft weapons said to hum with inner fire. |
| 8,000 | **Khar’zul** | Built directly above flowing magma channels. The ground pulses with heat. Fiercely isolationist, ruled by forge-clans. |
| 7,900 | **Vraelen** | City of ash farmers and sandwrights — specialists in crafting soil from volcanic glass. Surrounded by black terraces. |
| 7,400 | **Pyrrath** | Center for scholars of elemental lore. Its libraries are carved into basalt cliffs where molten light runs through crystal conduits. |
| 5,700 (Seaport) | **Duskreach** | Small but vital southern port. Known for its eerie glow — lanterns are fueled by gas vents instead of oil. Gateway for trade with the Ssil’ka. |
| 5,100 | **Zhoran** | A militant mining city whose people are known as “the Ashborn Legion.” Supplies iron and obsidian to half the Marches. |
| 5,000 | **Fyrvale** | Named for the valley of constant lightning storms it occupies. Holds sacred dueling grounds where Emberborn test their worth. |
| 4,500 | **Orrvak** | Hidden city built inside a collapsed caldera. The forges here produce enchanted chains and bindings. |
| 2,200 | **Cairnfall** | Built near the ruins of a mountain temple buried by eruption centuries ago. Home to mystics and hermits. |
| 1,800 | **Eshkaal** | A mining hamlet that produces sulfur and rare volcanic salts. Known for its fiery springs and isolation. |
| 1,700 | **Mordrith** | A frontier village clinging to the edge of the molten badlands. Its people are rugged salvagers and scavengers of old war machines. |
| 1,500 | **Taarvek** | A small but ancient settlement around a still-active lava spire. |
| 1,400 | **Draveth** | A pilgrimage site of the Cycle of Ash; priests light their torches here before traveling to Thurakar. |
| 1,000 | **Iskar’s Hollow** | A dying hamlet built in the cooled bones of an ancient lava flow. Once prosperous, now half-buried under drifting ash. |
