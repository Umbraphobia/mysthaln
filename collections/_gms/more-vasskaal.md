# **Cities of Vass’Kaal**

| **City Name** | **Population** | **Details** |
|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Ssa’Ruun** | 25,000 | Capital and great seaport of Vass’Kaal. A vast city built on canals and elevated walkways that wind through mangrove-choked deltas. The royal brood of the Ssil’ka resides within the Shellspire Palace — a living coral fortress that grows each generation. |
| **Hisskaar** | 18,000 | Inland trade city and spiritual center where the Ssil’ka shamans commune with the jungle spirits. Known for its great ziggurat covered in glowing moss. |
| **Vash’tel** | 17,000 | Industrial hub deep within the tropical interior. Ironwood and stone are shaped here into the towering architecture found throughout Vass’Kaal. |
| **Ssil’thar** | 14,000 | Jungle city built into cliffs above a mist-filled gorge. Famous for its venom brewers and alchemists. |
| **Kaassha** | 13,000 | River city known for breeding giant amphibians and scaled beasts that serve as steeds and guardians. |
| **Issh’ra** | 12,000 | Mountain-edge city where stone meets jungle. Its temples are carved directly into obsidian cliffs. |
| **Hra’Vesh** | 12,000 | Trade city connecting the western marshlands to Dun’Rhael’s border. Known for its amphibian-mounted patrols. |
| **Saar’thul** | 10,000 | Seaport city and shipyard that produces shallow-draft river vessels and scale-armored warships. |
| **Vash’Kael** | 10,000 | Jungle market city famed for its silk and scalecraft — textiles woven from shed reptilian hides. |
| **Ssisra** | 9,500 | Southern city with vast hatcheries where bonded reptiles are raised and trained for companionship or war. |
| **Thraash** | 9,400 | Cliffside fortress guarding one of the main inland passes. Built from black stone and surrounded by carnivorous flora. |
| **Hss’Vel** | 9,000 | City of scholars and serpent-keepers. Houses the **Ophidian Archive**, a living library where serpents carry engraved metal scales of written record. |
| **Kra’nash** | 6,500 | Riverside trade city surrounded by flooded plains and rice paddies. Known for its floating markets. |
| **Ssil’karr** | 6,500 | Sacred site where the first Ssil’ka bonds were formed with the reptilian spirits. A place of pilgrimage. |
| **Ssha’ven** | 6,400 | Seaport city along the southern coast, known for pearl diving and coral harvesting. Its streets shimmer with bioluminescent shells. |
| **Thrassal** | 4,200 | Seaport town at the mouth of the Emerald River, serving as a link between inland trade routes and coastal merchants. |
| **Issa’nar** | 3,200 | Inland city surrounded by thick jungle. Its elevated homes are built atop colossal tree roots. |
| **Kra’shel** | 2,600 | Agricultural town that breeds amphibians and raises medicinal plants in flooded terraces. |
| **Vas’shaar** | 2,400 | River confluence settlement where healing tonics and antidotes are traded. |
| **Hss’kal** | 2,300 | Seaport known for its shrine to the Tide Serpent, protector of sailors and bonders alike. |
| **Thiss’ra** | 1,800 | Seaport town with a labyrinthine harbor of hidden caves used by smugglers and sea priests. |
| **Ssha’neth** | 1,800 | Inland village built upon pillars to survive the seasonal floods. Renowned for breeding small scaled familiars. |
| **Ssil’ven** | 1,600 | Cliff village overlooking a sacred waterfall. Known for meditation shrines and singing stones. |
| **Vesh’taal** | 1,500 | Small inland town centered around a deep cenote believed to connect to the underworld. |
| **Ssha’rin** | 1,500 | Jungle-edge town built near a natural hot spring that glows faintly at night. |
| **Hra’ssil** | 1,400 | Northern jungle outpost near the border with Dun’Rhael. A haven for scouts and trackers. |
| **Ka’thesh** | 1,400 | Secluded research enclave where venomologists study the toxins of rare serpents. |
| **Thra’kaal** | 1,400 | Southern coastal town perched on high rocks, known for its stormwatchers and sea-scale fishers. |
