## Full List of Cities

# **Cities of Njorath**

| City Name | Population | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| **Korrveld** | 16,000 | Capital city of Njorath; center of governance and the seat of the High Clans. |
| **Vaerik’s Rest** | 13,000 | Major seaport and shipyard; known for its black-iron vessels and long-prowed warships. |
| **Haldren** | 9,500 | Inland stronghold; major producer of timber and iron. |
| **Draskar** | 8,900 | Trade town along the western routes into Drenvold; famed for its hardy craftsmanship. |
| **Tirnhold** | 8,700 | Old fortress city built into a cliffside; guards the northern passes. |
| **Marhavn** | 7,900 | Seaport; hub of northern fishing and trade with Skarhold. |
| **Velgrad** | 7,500 | Cultural and spiritual center of the Njorathi people; home of the ancient Stone Choir. |
| **Ornkaal** | 5,100 | Known for blacksmithing and arms production; supplies much of Njorath’s militia. |
| **Drenfyr** | 4,300 | Forest settlement renowned for its bowyers and woodcarvers. |
| **Falrek** | 4,200 | Seaport; key naval outpost facing the northern sea. |
| **Skarnhollow** | 2,400 | Seaport; small but vital for ore exports and coastal patrols. |
| **Vaelgard** | 1,800 | Mining village built near deep mountain veins. |
| **Thornmere** | 1,700 | Forest town situated near ancient ruins of the First Age. |
| **Grimholt** | 1,500 | Harsh northern village known for tanners and furriers. |
| **Ravik’s Den** | 1,500 | Remote border hamlet near Drenvold; a haven for hunters and smugglers. |
