# **Cities and Towns of Vardessa**

| City / Town | Population | Notes |
|--------------|-------------|-------|
| **Vareth** | 23,000 | Capital; fortified inland city built atop the ruins of a Supremacy outpost; center of governance and resistance. |
| **Cair Hollow** | 21,000 | Once a Supremacy garrison, now a major military stronghold; houses the War Council of Vardessa. |
| **Tirvale** | 20,000 | Industrial hub producing weapons and siege engines; sustained by nearby iron and coal deposits. |
| **Eldross** | 18,000 | Cultural and political heart of the southern provinces; home to scholars and historians of the split. |
| **Rathmoor** | 14,000 | Key agricultural region supplying the northern front; heavily fortified against Supremacy raids. |
| **Hareth’s Crossing** | 14,000 | Strategic city guarding the western border near Dun’Rhael; a critical crossroads for trade and armies. |
| **Korrinhold** | 13,000 | Major inland fortress-city and site of several key battles against the Supremacy. |
| **Vaelrun** | 11,000 | Known for its great library and archives of the pre-secession era; often targeted by spies and saboteurs. |
| **Marra’s Gate** | 10,000 | Western trade city linking Dun’Rhael and Vardessa through caravan routes. |
| **Stoneveil** | 9,800 | Mining settlement nestled in the northern foothills; produces stone and iron for Vardessa’s war effort. |
| **Kethar’s Rest** | 9,100 | Seaport; a bustling naval harbor and shipyard for the Vardessian fleet. |
| **Thyrenfell** | 8,600 | Farming and supply hub; specializes in horses and war beasts for the army. |
| **Redhaven** | 8,400 | Seaport; known for shipwrights and smugglers who ferry goods through Supremacy blockades. |
| **Tarrin’s Ford** | 8,000 | Bridge city over the River Velth; an important choke point and trade crossing. |
| **Vaelorin** | 6,700 | Quiet scholarly town and monastery dedicated to the Flame of Renewal. |
| **Brenvale** | 5,600 | Central marketplace for the surrounding farmlands. |
| **Ironmere** | 5,300 | Mining and blacksmithing town; produces high-grade steel for armor and weapons. |
| **Kelross** | 5,000 | Administrative center managing supply lines to the northern front. |
| **Drayfen** | 4,800 | Known for its network of tunnels and hidden forges used during the rebellion. |
| **Lowmere** | 2,700 | Small lakeside town that trades fish and fresh water with the inland cities. |
| **Orrin’s Wake** | 2,300 | Frontier town near the border with Drenvold; often attacked but never taken. |
| **Farrowfen** | 2,200 | Swampland settlement used as a hidden refuge and training ground for scouts. |
| **Lareth’s Hold** | 1,700 | Small inland keep that guards the approach to Vaelrun. |
| **Tern’s Watch** | 1,700 | Coastal watchtower settlement; monitors naval movements from Skarhold and the Supremacy. |
| **Myrrenfall** | 1,700 | River town with fertile farmland and watermills. |
| **Korras Deep** | 1,700 | Subterranean mining outpost along the northern hills. |
| **Aven’s Gate** | 1,600 | Small fortress settlement near the eastern trade road. |
| **Westmere** | 1,600 | Border village serving as a trade and intelligence hub with Dun’Rhael. |
| **Silverfen** | 1,600 | Known for rare herbs and alchemical reagents harvested from nearby wetlands. |
| **Torren’s Bridge** | 1,600 | Crossing town built over an ancient Supremacy aqueduct. |
| **Darrow Hill** | 1,500 | Agricultural hamlet supporting Vareth’s food supply. |
| **Hollowmar** | 1,500 | Hidden town built into the cliffs overlooking the southern marches. |
| **Isenvale** | 1,500 | Logging settlement supplying timber for ships and siege engines. |
| **Korrin’s Rest** | 1,400 | Small fortified village with a shrine to the fallen heroes of the secession. |
| **Waveharbor** | 1,100 | Seaport; smallest of the coastal towns, known for smuggling and refuge for deserters. |
